## Premises

- <span style="color:#1a7f37;font-weight:bold">I like software.</span> I like to understand how it works, what is behind it, and where the value is. I put the effort in.
- <span style="color:#1a7f37;font-weight:bold">I'm good with software.</span> These are skills that translate. I can be the tech person for someone, for a business. People need this.
- <span style="color:#1a7f37;font-weight:bold">There might be a need for it.</span> Freelance, side jobs, custom apps. For that I need something legal to declare the work through and give it structure.
- <span style="color:#1a7f37;font-weight:bold">Owning a business is good.</span> It's mine. I work for myself. Most of what I win is mine (minus the taxes, of course). It also costs <span style="color:#b42318;font-weight:bold">time and effort</span>.
- Software is <span style="color:#b42318;font-weight:bold">competitive</span>. The people in it are smart. Hard market to break into. It is also in <span style="color:#1a7f37;font-weight:bold">constant change</span>. What's real today is not real tomorrow. If you're good, fast, and putting the work in, that's an opening.

## Project

- **RCELO-SOFTWARE** is born as a software studio.
- On **17 January 2025**, the [website](https://rcelo-software.com/) went live.

- The name is <span style="color:#b42318;font-weight:bold">hard to pronounce</span> and the branding is <span style="color:#b42318;font-weight:bold">not that good</span>.
- <span style="color:#1a7f37;font-weight:bold">But I like it.</span> The name comes from my hometown, Barcelos. That's why the logo is a rooster. I am representing!

And because of that branding: if I ever land business, you'll know they're here because I was good, not because of the brand.

Almost two years in and I only built this project page now, while updating my personal website. Talk about technical debt.

It's been a while. Still a draft. Zero clients, zero revenue. Honestly, I drifted. Some months I worked on it, some months I just forgot about it.

Still solo. When I say "we", I mean me.

I built a few prototypes. Nothing polished, nothing ready for a real client. I poked at different tools and stacks just to see what's out there.

**Slowly and steady wins the race.**
